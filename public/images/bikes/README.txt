DROP YOUR BIKE IMAGES HERE
==========================

Replace these placeholder paths by saving JPG/PNG/WEBP files with the EXACT
filenames listed below. Wide product photography (16:10 or 16:9) on a dark
background works best.

Required filenames (lowercase, hyphenated):

  ventus-v1-plus.jpg
  talaria-mx5.jpg
  sur-ron-light-bee-x.jpg
  e-ride-pro-ss.jpg
  rawrr-mantis-x.jpg
  arctic-leopard-exe.jpg
  stealth-bomber-3000w.jpg

If a filename does not exist yet, the site will automatically fall back to a
clean animated placeholder card so the layout still looks good.

To add a brand-new bike:
  1. Drop the image in this folder.
  2. Open /lib/products.ts and add a new entry to the `products` array.
